# Participant Scenario Card
### ORIGON Mindfulness Coaching Study

---

## Your Scenario

You are a **second-year university student** in the middle of the semester.
Play this role during the conversation. Speak naturally — do not read lines out loud.

---

## Your Situation

- You have **two assignments due this week** and a quiz coming up.
- You have been telling yourself and others that you are *fine* and handling it.
- But honestly, you have **not been sleeping well** — you stay up past midnight most nights watching videos to unwind.
- You have been **procrastinating** on the assignments and feel a low-level stress you have not fully admitted to yourself yet.
- You tried a breathing exercise a friend showed you once — it helped a little but you forgot to keep doing it.

---

## How the Conversation Should Go

Follow these beats loosely. Use your own words.

| Turn | What to do |
|------|-----------|
| 1 | Respond to the greeting casually. Say things are pretty good, nothing major. |
| 2 | When asked what's going on, say you have a couple of assignments but it's manageable. |
| 3 | Mention you've been sleeping fine when the topic comes up — you're used to late nights. |
| 4 | Admit that the procrastination has been getting worse. You keep opening your laptop and doing nothing. |
| 5 | If the agent suggests something, say you'll try it. |
| 6 | **Contradict yourself** — mention you've actually been sleeping pretty badly, going to bed around 1–2am most nights. You know it's the videos. |
| 7 | Respond to whatever the agent picks up on. Be honest — things are a bit more stressful than you let on at the start. |
| 8 | Say you feel a bit better after talking and wrap up. |

---

## Key Contradiction (Important)

At **Turn 3** say sleep is *fine*.
At **Turn 6** admit sleep has actually been *bad*.

This shift is intentional — let it happen naturally mid-conversation, as if you just remembered or decided to be more honest.

---

## Reminders

- Speak at a normal pace. Wait for the agent to finish before replying.
- You do not need to say anything clever — plain, casual speech works best.
- If the agent mishears you, just repeat yourself.
- End the session by saying *"bye"* or *"I'm done"*.

---

*Session length: approximately 5–8 minutes.*
